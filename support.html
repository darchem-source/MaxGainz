Common questions

How do I reset my password?
  1. On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password and enter your account email.
  2. Open the email we send you on your iPhone and tap the link. It opens Max Gainz.
  3. Choose a new password and sign in.

No email? Check spam, make sure you used the email you signed up with, and wait a few minutes. If it still does not arrive, email us.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not inside Max Gainz. On your iPhone open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Max Gainz and tap Cancel Subscription (or Cancel Free Trial). Do this at least 24 hours before the renewal or trial end date. You can also go to apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles refunds. Go to support.apple.com/HT204084 and follow the steps. We are unable to refund purchases made through the App Store.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Install Max Gainz, sign in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe, and tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen [or Settings → Subscription → Restore Purchases]. If your subscription does not appear, email us.

How do I export my data?

Go to [Settings → Data → Export] and choose where to save or share the file. Export is available at all times, including after your trial or subscription ends.

How do I delete my account?

Go to [Settings → Account → Delete Account] and confirm. This permanently deletes your sign-in, profile, workouts and linked feedback. It cannot be undone, so export your data first if you want a copy. You can do this even without an active subscription.

Your Apple subscription is not cancelled by deleting your account. Cancel it in iPhone Settings first (see above).

Can't get into the app? Email us from your account email address and we will delete it for you.

Why am I not getting training reminders?

These need notification permission. Open Settings → Notifications → Max Gainz on your iPhone and turn on Allow Notifications. Also check that Focus modes are not hiding them, and that reminders are switched on inside Max Gainz settings (one reminder in the morning when a muscle group is overdue). Notifications are optional; the app works without them.

My trial ended. What can I still do?

You can view your workout history, export your data and delete your account. To log new workouts you need an active subscription. Your history is kept.

Can I use Max Gainz without an account?

Yes. Choose Continue without an account. Your data then stays on your iPhone only and is lost if you delete the app, so create an account if you want sync and backup.

Safety

Max Gainz is a training log, not medical advice. Stop exercising if you feel pain and speak to a medical professional. See the Terms of Use.
