3. No analytics, advertising or tracking

Max Gainz does not use any analytics, advertising, attribution or tracking software development kits (SDKs). We do not show ads. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not share your data with data brokers. The app does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission because it does not track.

If you opt in to sharing diagnostics and usage data with developers in your iPhone's privacy settings, Apple may provide us with aggregated, de-identified App Store statistics and crash reports. We cannot identify you from them.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account, profile and workout data: for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account we delete this data straight away from our live systems, and from backups within [30] days.
  • Feedback: linked to your account while it exists and deleted with it. Feedback sent anonymously cannot be linked to you; we keep it for up to [24 months] to improve the app.
  • Subscription records: the record held by RevenueCat is deleted when you delete your account. Apple keeps its own purchase records under its own policies, and we may keep non-personal accounting totals as the law requires.
  • Server logs: kept by Supabase and Netlify for the periods they set, normally short (days to a few weeks).
  • Guest data: stays only on your device until you create an account. Deleting the app removes it.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account inside the app: [Settings → Account → Delete Account]. This removes your sign-in record, profile (including photo), workout history, notes and any feedback linked to your account. You do not need to contact us, and you can delete your account even if your trial or subscription has ended.

Subscriptions are separate. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Billing continues through Apple until you cancel in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions (or at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions). The app reminds you of this before you confirm deletion. You can export your data first from within the app.

If you cannot use the app, email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from your account email address and we will delete the account for you.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • be told how your data is used (this policy);
  • access your data. The in-app export gives you your workout history and profile;
  • have inaccurate data corrected (you can edit most of it in the app);
  • have your data erased (see section 8);
  • restrict or object to some uses, including uses based on legitimate interests;
  • receive your data in a portable format (the in-app export);
  • withdraw consent at any time where we rely on it.

To use a right that the app does not cover, email [SUPPORT EMAIL]. We will reply within one month. We may need to confirm your identity first, and we will not charge a fee unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive.

10. Complaints

Please contact us first so we can try to put things right. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, telephone 0303 123 1113.

11. Security

Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Access to each account's data is restricted to that account by database rules. No online service is perfectly secure, so please choose a strong, unique password. If a data breach is likely to put you at risk, we will tell you and the ICO as the law requires.

12. Children

Max Gainz is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you are under 13, please do not create an account. If we learn we hold an account belonging to a child under 13, we will delete it. The app is a general health and wellness app and is not a medical device.
